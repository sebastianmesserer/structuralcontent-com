Sending us a data snapshot

You send us exports from the systems that hold the story behind your business priority. We send back campaign briefs for your team. This page shows how we keep that safe, and everything your IT or privacy team will want to check. For your pilot you get your own version, with your sources and your column lists.

Why this is safe for you

You choose what leaves
You export only the columns on our list. Names, email addresses and your colleagues' names stay in your system. We set up the export with you once, in a 15-minute screen-share.
It stays in the EU
Your file is stored in Frankfurt, read on an encrypted company laptop that stays in the EU, and analysed by AI in EU data centres. It is used for one thing: your briefs. The AI service keeps nothing and learns nothing from it.
It gets deleted
We delete your data and everything made from it within 30 days of the pilot ending, and confirm that to you in writing.
It is covered by contract
A data processing agreement (AVV), the same paperwork your company signs for any software tool. Ours comes filled in, ready to sign.

Which columns

Which systems matter depends on your priority: CRM, call recordings, support, product usage, surveys, chat or project tools, or whatever else holds the story. For each one you get a column list, and it doubles as the checklist we use to build the export with you. Every column falls into one of five groups:

GroupExamples
Business factsincludeStages, amounts, dates, categories, tags, reasons won or lost, plan or segment, usage counts, company name
IDsincludeRecord and company IDs, so records from different systems can be linked
PeopleexcludeNames, email addresses, phone numbers of contacts and customers
Your staffexcludeOwner, assignee, created-by, team or agent fields
Free textyour choiceNotes, descriptions, transcripts, comments

Free text makes the briefs noticeably better, because we can quote what customers actually said. It can also contain names someone typed in. You decide per source.

Because no staff fields are exported, the pilot cannot be used to monitor your employees' performance. In German companies that usually keeps the works council (§ 87(1) no. 6 BetrVG) out of scope.

Questions reviewers usually ask

Is the export anonymised?

No. Removing names makes it pseudonymised, which is still personal data under GDPR. We treat it that way, which is why the AVV applies.

Who can see our data?

Named Structural Content staff, for the purpose of running your pilot. Currently that is one person, the managing director. The AI service processes it in memory and keeps nothing.

Does the AI provider train on our data?

No. Amazon Bedrock does not use inputs or outputs to train models, and our account is set to zero data retention.

Does data leave the EU?

No. Storage is in Frankfurt, the company laptop that reads the files stays in the EU, and AI inference uses AWS's EU cross-region profile, which routes only within EU regions.

Do you need access to our systems?

No. You export files and upload them. There are no logins, API keys or connectors.

What if we stop the pilot early?

Tell us and we delete within 30 days of that date, and confirm in writing.

Can we audit you?

Yes, under the AVV. You can also ask for the snapshot log at any time.

Was Prüfer:innen meist fragen

Ist der Export anonymisiert?

Nein. Ohne Namen ist er pseudonymisiert, und das bleibt nach DSGVO personenbezogen. Wir behandeln ihn so, deshalb gilt der AVV.

Wer kann unsere Daten sehen?

Benannte Mitarbeitende von Structural Content, zum Zweck Ihres Pilots. Derzeit ist das eine Person, der Geschäftsführer. Der KI-Dienst verarbeitet die Daten im Arbeitsspeicher und speichert nichts.

Trainiert der KI-Anbieter mit unseren Daten?

Nein. Amazon Bedrock nutzt Ein- und Ausgaben nicht zum Training, und unser Konto ist auf Zero Data Retention eingestellt.

Verlassen Daten die EU?

Nein. Gespeichert wird in Frankfurt, der Firmen-Laptop, der die Dateien liest, bleibt in der EU, und die KI-Inferenz nutzt das EU-Inferenzprofil von AWS, das nur innerhalb von EU-Regionen routet.

Brauchen Sie Zugang zu unseren Systemen?

Nein. Sie exportieren Dateien und laden sie hoch. Keine Logins, keine API-Schlüssel, keine Konnektoren.

Was, wenn wir den Pilot früher beenden?

Sagen Sie Bescheid, dann löschen wir innerhalb von 30 Tagen ab diesem Datum und bestätigen es schriftlich.

Dürfen wir Sie prüfen?

Ja, nach dem AVV. Das Protokoll der Auszüge können Sie jederzeit anfordern.
