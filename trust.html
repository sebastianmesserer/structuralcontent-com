Our commitments

Your data serves your briefs

We use what you share for one purpose: the briefs for your company.

It stays in the EU

Stored in Frankfurt and analysed by AI in EU data centres.

You choose what we see

You pick the systems and the columns. Names and email addresses stay in your systems, apart from what free-text fields hold if you choose to send them.

Zero retention at the AI

The AI service runs with zero data retention: it keeps your inputs and outputs only while it works on them.

Deleted when we are done

Your data and everything made from it is deleted within 30 days of the work ending, confirmed in writing.

A standard contract

The EU Commission's standard data processing clauses (Art. 28 GDPR), the form your privacy team already knows.

Before the first call

The first call is about your strategic priority and which of your systems hold the story behind it. Data comes later, once you decide to go ahead and the data processing agreement is in place.

You then start with file exports you create yourself, from the systems you choose. Logins, API keys and connectors stay with you.

Questions teams ask before a call

Do you need access to our systems?

You start with file exports you create yourself. Logins, API keys and connectors stay with you. Connecting your systems is a later step, agreed with you separately.

Is our data used to train AI models?

Your data serves your briefs only. Amazon Bedrock uses inputs and outputs to produce the response, and our account runs with zero data retention.

Where is our data processed?

In the EU: storage in Frankfurt, AI inference in EU regions through AWS's EU cross-region profile, and the work on a company laptop that stays in the EU.

Who at Structural Content can see it?

Named staff bound to confidentiality, for your work only. Today that is one person, the managing director.

Which data would we send?

That depends on your priority: CRM, call recordings, support, product usage, surveys, or whatever else holds the story. For each system we agree a column list with you. It includes business facts such as stages, amounts, dates and reasons, and leaves names, email addresses and your colleagues' names in your system. Free-text fields such as notes or transcripts are your choice per system; they make the briefs better and can contain names someone typed in.

Does our works council need to be involved?

Without free-text fields, your staff's data stays in your systems. In German companies that usually keeps the work outside § 87(1) no. 6 BetrVG. If you include free text such as call transcripts, we help you remove agent names first.

What happens to our data at the end?

We delete it, and everything made from it, within 30 days of the work ending, and confirm it to you in writing. You can end the work at any time by email.

Do you hold ISO 27001 or SOC 2?

In place of a certificate, you get the full data processing agreement and technical measures to review directly, and you can audit us under the agreement.

How do we report a security concern?

Email sebastian@structuralcontent.com, the managing director, who handles it personally.
