How it works

From your number to the content that moves it.

1

Your number

The number that matters right now, and where it needs to be. For example: 120 free teams upgrading a month today, 200 by March.

2

Who has to act

It works out which people that number depends on: the trial users, the buyers who approve, the teammates they invite.

3

Where they get stuck, and why

It reads everything you have on them, from sales calls and support tickets to how they use your product, and finds the groups that stall and what stops them.

4

What to make

Each stall becomes a campaign brief in the tools your team already uses, with a target for that group’s rate.

5

And again

As campaigns go live and the data moves, it finds where people get stuck next.

Why it differs

It finds what a team can’t.

AI writing tools help you make content faster, once you know what to make. Structural Content helps you know what to make in the first place.

A team can read a sample of calls and tickets when it has time. Structural Content reads all of it, all the time, and finds the specific groups and reasons a sample misses.

And it doesn’t stop at one report. As your data changes, so do the problems it shows you.

Who it’s for

Who it’s for.

B2B software companies working on pipeline, activation, retention or expansion, where too many people stall somewhere between first contact and renewal.

It’s for whoever owns that number: often the founder while growth is still founder-led, a marketing lead once one is in place.

It works best when your customers leave a trail: a CRM, call recordings, support conversations, product usage. You don’t need a content team. You need someone who can make the content, in-house, freelance or with AI.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does it get set up in my company?

It starts with a call about your number and where people stall today. If it’s worth taking further, we run a pilot on exports from your own data. Nothing connects to your live systems at that stage.

Once the pilot has proven its value, Structural Content runs as a dedicated instance for your company. It connects to the systems your customers leave a trail in, from your CRM to calls, support and product usage, and keeps looking as the data changes.

What exactly does it produce?

A short, ranked list of campaign briefs. Each one says:

  • who it’s for, and where they’re stuck
  • the evidence for why, from your own data
  • what to make, piece by piece
  • the target for that group’s rate, and how you’ll know it worked

The briefs arrive in the tools your team already uses. To see example briefs for your own goal, try the demo above.

Does it also write the content?

No. It decides what is worth making and why.

Each brief carries business context a writer rarely gets: who exactly the people are, what stops them, in their own words, and what has to change for the number to move. Whoever makes it, your team, a freelancer or an AI model, knows exactly what the piece has to do.

Does this create more work for my team?

No. It replaces loosely aimed content with precise work that moves your number, and because each brief carries the full context, that work is easier to make.

Can’t we just do this with Claude, or build it ourselves?

You could get part of the way, with a chat model, a CRM export, an automation tool and someone’s spare hours.

The hard part isn’t access to a model. It’s reading all of your data together, every call, ticket and record, to find the specific groups and reasons, then ranking them by what moves your number, and doing that again as the data changes. That’s what Structural Content is built to do.

How do I know my data is secure?

The baseline for a pilot: you choose what to share, and nothing connects to your live systems. Pilot data is processed in the EU only, under a data processing agreement signed before anything is shared, and it is never used to train AI models. We take you through the full setup on the first call.
